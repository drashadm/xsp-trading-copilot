# Report Examples

This index points to synthetic public-safe examples that demonstrate replayability, no-lookahead review, alert suppression, and simplified app posture.

## Examples

* [Replay Report Sample](../examples/replay_report_sample.md): shows session review, evidence quality, opportunity flags, missing evidence gaps, and lessons learned.
* [Point-in-Time Rehearsal Sample](../examples/point_in_time_rehearsal_sample.md): shows no-lookahead candidate generation from evidence available at a specific timestamp.
* [Alert Outbox Sample](../examples/alert_outbox_sample.json): shows eligible dry-run, cooldown suppression, stale-evidence suppression, and duplicate-transition suppression.
* [Public App State Sample](../examples/public_app_state_sample.json): shows a simplified external-facing posture without raw private data.

## Warning

All examples are synthetic and public-safe. They are not real trading logs, broker records, screenshots, or performance reports. They should not be interpreted as financial advice, financial performance claims, or proof that the system predicted a market move.
